# 🎵 SQL Music Store Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
The **SQL Music Store Analysis Project** analyzes a fictional music store database to answer real-world business questions using **SQL**.  
The project demonstrates hands-on experience with **joins, aggregations, filtering, sorting, CTEs, and window functions** to generate actionable business insights related to **customers, sales, artists, genres, and geography**.

## 🗂 Dataset Structure
The database consists of the following tables:

- **employee** – Employee details including job title and seniority  
- **customer** – Customer information  
- **invoice** – Invoice-level billing data  
- **invoice_line** – Detailed invoice items  
- **track** – Track details including duration and genre  
- **album** – Album information  
- **artist** – Artist details  
- **genre** – Genre classification  

Tables are connected using primary and foreign keys such as  
`customer_id`, `invoice_id`, `track_id`, `album_id`, and `artist_id`.

## 🧠 Business Questions Solved

### 🟢 Easy Level
1. Find the most senior employee based on job title  
2. Identify countries with the highest number of invoices  
3. Find the top 3 invoice totals  
4. Determine the city with the highest total invoice amount  
5. Identify the customer who has spent the most money  

---

### 🟡 Moderate Level
1. Retrieve customers who listen to **Rock** music  
2. Identify the **top 10 rock artists** based on track count  
3. Find tracks longer than the average track length  

---

### 🔴 Advanced Level
1. Calculate customer spending on each artist  
2. Identify the most popular music genre by country  
3. Find the top-spending customer for each country  

CTEs and window functions are used extensively for advanced analysis.
